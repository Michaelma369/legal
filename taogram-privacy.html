Privacy Policy

TAOGRAM · Last updated: 17 November 2026

TAOGRAM is a piece of interactive folk art that runs entirely on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and there is no account to create.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, or usage data.

What stays on your device

  • Your written intention. The phrase you type is stored only in the app's local database on your device, and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Ritual progress. The seven-day state of each paper is stored locally. A small read-only snapshot is shared with the TAOGRAM home-screen widget through an Apple App Group container on the same device.
  • Your seal image. A 512 × 512 image is generated on your device. It leaves the app only when you tap Share or Save to Photos.

Permissions and why

  • Location (while using the app). Used only to read the compass heading during the seventh-day step, so the on-screen compass can follow the direction you turn. No coordinate is requested, stored, or transmitted.
  • Microphone. Used only during the ash step to detect gentle airflow so the ash can be blown away. Audio is measured in memory and discarded immediately. Nothing is recorded, saved, or transmitted.
  • Photos (add only). Used only when you tap Save to Photos to export your seal or your film.
  • Notifications. Used only for the single local reminder on the seventh day. No push server is involved.

You can decline any permission. The ritual remains completable: an on-screen alternative is provided for the compass, and the ash can also be cleared with a swipe.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's handling of that data is described in Apple's own Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
