隐私政策（中文）

本政策适用于「谷美物语」（英文名 CardCam，以下简称「本 App」）。请在使用前阅读。

四句话说完：
  • 你的照片只存在你自己的手机里，我们看不到。
  • 我们不设账号、不采集照片内容；只收集不含身份信息的匿名使用统计，用来改进产品。
  • 相机与相册权限，只用于拍摄和保存你的出片。
  • 你删除照片，它就从设备上被销毁，我们无从恢复。

一、我们收集什么

本 App 不设账号、不需注册、不接入登录服务。你的照片、谷仓记录、本命清单、签文记录，全部保存在设备本地存储与 App 沙盒目录中，不会离开你的设备。

我们只收集匿名使用统计，用来判断哪些功能有人用、哪里会出错：

  • 事件名：例如「打开印刷预览」「保存成片」。
  • 少量业务参数：例如选用的模板 / 滤镜名称、张数、页数、耗时。
  • 随机匿名 ID：首次启动时在你的设备上随机生成的一串字符，不是设备标识，不含任何个人信息。
  • 时间戳，以及服务器收到的请求 IP（仅用于得出大致国家 / 城市，不做定位，不用于识别个人）。

以上内容不包含你的照片、照片内容、文件名、文件路径、通讯录、定位或设备名，也不与你的身份关联。

二、权限用途

  • 相机：仅在你主动进入拍摄界面时启用，用于取景与拍照。不在后台调用。
  • 相册读取：仅在你主动选择「旧谷返新」导入已有照片时读取你选中的那一张。
  • 相册写入：仅在你点击保存时，将成片写入系统相册。

以上权限均可在系统设置中随时关闭。关闭后相关功能不可用，但本 App 其余部分仍可正常使用。

三、数据存储与删除

所有数据存储于设备本地。你可以在 App 内删除任意照片与记录；卸载本 App 将一并清除其沙盒内的全部数据。由于我们不持有任何副本，删除后无法找回，请谨慎操作。

四、第三方服务

我们使用以下第三方服务，除此之外没有别的：

  • PostHog（美国 · us.i.posthog.com）：接收上述匿名使用统计。不用于广告，不用于跨 App 或跨网站追踪，数据不出售。
  • Apple App Store：内购交易与收据由 Apple 处理，我们拿不到你的姓名、支付卡号或账单地址。
  • RevenueCat：用于校验购买状态与恢复购买。它接收一个匿名的 App 用户 ID 和购买凭证，同样拿不到你的支付信息。

本 App 不含广告 SDK，也不含崩溃上报 SDK。

五、儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童信息。本 App 不采集可识别身份的信息，匿名统计同样不含身份信息，因此不存在此类风险。

六、你的权利（GDPR / CCPA）

若你位于欧洲经济区或美国加利福尼亚州，相关法规赋予你访问、更正、删除个人数据及拒绝出售个人信息的权利。我们持有的数据只有两类：留在你设备上的内容（我们不持有副本），以及不含身份信息的匿名使用统计。我们不出售任何个人信息，也不做跨 App 追踪。如希望查询或删除匿名统计记录，请通过第八节联系我们。

七、政策变更

如本政策发生变更，我们将更新本页顶部的「最后更新」日期。重大变更会在 App 内提示。

八、联系我们

任何隐私相关问题，请联系：michael.ma3339@gmail.com

Privacy Policy (English)

This policy applies to CardCam (Chinese name: 谷美物语, hereinafter "the App"). Please read it before use.

In four sentences:
  • Your photos stay on your own device. We never see them.
  • No accounts, no photo content. We only collect anonymous usage statistics with no identity attached, to improve the App.
  • Camera and photo library permissions are used solely to capture and save your images.
  • When you delete a photo, it is destroyed on your device. We hold no copy and cannot recover it.

1. What We Collect

The App has no accounts, no registration, and no sign-in services. Your photos, collection records, wishlist, and fortune history are stored entirely in local device storage and the App's sandbox directory. None of it leaves your device.

We collect only anonymous usage statistics, to see which features are used and where things break:

  • Event names — e.g. "print preview opened", "image saved".
  • A few business parameters — e.g. the template or filter name, card count, page count, duration.
  • A random anonymous ID — a string generated on your device at first launch. It is not a device identifier and contains no personal information.
  • A timestamp, plus the request IP seen by our analytics server (used only to derive an approximate country/city — not for location tracking, not to identify anyone).

None of this includes your photos, photo content, file names, file paths, contacts, location, or device name, and none of it is linked to your identity.

2. Permission Usage

  • Camera — activated only when you actively open the capture screen, for framing and taking photos. Never accessed in the background.
  • Photo Library (read) — reads only the single image you explicitly select when importing an existing photo.
  • Photo Library (write) — writes your finished image to the system library only when you tap save.

All permissions can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings. The related features will become unavailable; the rest of the App continues to work.

3. Data Storage and Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You may delete any photo or record from within the App. Uninstalling the App removes all data in its sandbox. Because we hold no copies, deleted data cannot be recovered — please proceed with care.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, and no others:

  • PostHog (United States · us.i.posthog.com) — receives the anonymous usage statistics described above. Not used for advertising, not used for cross-app or cross-site tracking, never sold.
  • Apple App Store — in-app purchase transactions and receipts are handled by Apple. We never receive your name, card number, or billing address.
  • RevenueCat — validates purchase status and restores purchases. It receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt; it does not receive your payment details.

The App contains no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. The App collects no identifying information, and the anonymous statistics contain none either, so no such risk exists.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or in California, applicable law grants you rights to access, correct, and delete your personal data, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. We hold only two kinds of data: the content on your own device (we hold no copy), and anonymous usage statistics that carry no identity. We never sell personal information and never track you across apps. To enquire about or delete anonymous statistics, contact us using Section 8.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be surfaced within the App.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy-related question, contact: michael.ma3339@gmail.com
